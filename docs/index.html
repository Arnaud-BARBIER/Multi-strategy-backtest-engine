One core, three questions

The modes share the ledger, but not the question, and not the series that carries their performance. Reading the wrong series produces wrong numbers without looking wrong, so the mode decides it, never the caller.

Algorithmic

Signals, setups, regime routing, entries and exits under a realistic bar-level execution model. Does a rule have an edge?

carrier: closed trades

Allocation

Target weights, rebalance calendars, cash tiers, borrowing and fees. Does a weighting rule beat holding everything equally?

Investment
